Opening the Mac app the first time. This build is not notarized by Apple yet, so macOS shows a warning that it cannot check it for malicious software. The app is not damaged. To open it: drag PlanSaver to Applications and try to open it once, then click Done. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the Security section, click Open Anyway next to PlanSaver, and enter your password. On older macOS versions, right-click the app and choose Open instead. If you prefer Terminal: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PlanSaver.app. To check your download, its SHA-256 is 056046989402647348782111ef7f18d8f8f15515584f472680758c8f92fc8f39.

What it does

  • Compares US cell-phone plans for your lines, data and budget.
  • Shows phone deals, kept up to date by automated checks that refresh the deal list.
  • Also has Insurance, Phones and Cars tabs.
  • Built for Android and iOS.
