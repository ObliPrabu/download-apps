Opening the Mac app the first time. macOS will show a warning like “Apple could not verify ‘PlanSaver’ is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.” That is because this app is not notarized by Apple yet. It does not mean the app is damaged. To open it anyway:
  1. Drag PlanSaver to your Applications folder and open it.
  2. When the warning appears, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings and go to Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll down to the Security section. You will see a message that PlanSaver was blocked.
  5. Click Open Anyway, enter your Mac password, then click Open.
You only have to do this once. Prefer Terminal? Run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PlanSaver.app. To check your download, its SHA-256 is 056046989402647348782111ef7f18d8f8f15515584f472680758c8f92fc8f39.

What it does

  • Compares US cell-phone plans for your lines, data and budget.
  • Shows phone deals, kept up to date by automated checks that refresh the deal list.
  • Also has Insurance, Phones and Cars tabs.
  • Built for Android and iOS.
