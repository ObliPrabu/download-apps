Opening the Mac app the first time. macOS will show a warning like “Apple could not verify ‘BuddyVerse’ is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.” That is because this app is not notarized by Apple yet. It does not mean the app is damaged. To open it anyway:
  1. Drag BuddyVerse to your Applications folder and open it.
  2. When the warning appears, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings and go to Privacy & Security.
  4. Scroll down to the Security section. You will see a message that BuddyVerse was blocked.
  5. Click Open Anyway, enter your Mac password, then click Open.
You only have to do this once. Prefer Terminal? Run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/BuddyVerse.app. To check your download, its SHA-256 is 6a968d3201540a249ed1efbfcafa4f6bc6d9fcde683b2252bbaa2b38e4f2a609.

What is inside

  • Connect: 100+ jokes, 100+ riddles, 100+ trivia questions and conversation starters, all built in.
  • 20+ original mini-games: Tic-Tac-Toe, Sudoku, Hangman, Maze, Word Search, Memory Match and more, plus themed expedition games.
  • Play your way: one phone passed around, versus a bot, or online with a friend using a room code.
  • Privacy: no ads, no tracking across other apps, and the app asks for a first name once. Made for ages 13 and up.
